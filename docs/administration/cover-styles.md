# Cover styles

Every book has a cover. A book that came without one gets a **drawn cover**: its title, authors and series set in one
of several styles (Classic, Typographic, Geometric, Band, Minimal), each with several sets of colors. A book always
gets the same drawn cover, and neighbours on a shelf look different.

**Administration → Catalog → Cover styles** chooses which styles new books get. It needs the **Manage cover styles**
right ([rights](../reference/rights.md)), which Administrators and Curators have. The choice holds for the whole
tenant, every library.

## The page

- Each style shows a sample in each of its sets of colors, and a switch: **on**, new books may get it; **off**, they
  don't. One style always stays on.
- Turning a style off changes no book: the books already drawn in it keep it.
- Under a style turned off, **Give its books another style** gives each of its books one of the styles still on, after
  asking. It runs in the background (followed in the panel under the top bar and under
  [Background tasks](background-tasks.md)); scans and imports of the tenant wait for it to end. A style someone
  chose for a book on its page (**Cover** on the book's page) stays.

## On a book's page

**Cover** on a book's page offers the styles turned on, and the one the book has. A cover picked there stays through
scans, imports and the button above.
