# Finding your way around

## The top bar

- **Librarion** (the logo) goes back to the home page.
- **The library** you are browsing comes next. With more than one library, it is a button: pick another library from
  its list. The choice is kept by this browser. With a single library, its name is just shown.
- **Search** sits in the middle (it starts working with the search stage).
- **Your initial** at the right opens the account menu:
  - **My account:** your preferences and password ([your account](account.md)).
  - **Administration**, if your account may administer anything.
  - **Switch to light colors** / **Switch to dark colors**: for this browser only.
  - **Tenant**, for server administrators when there are several tenants: the one ticked is the one you work in.
  - **Sign out** of this browser.

## The navigation

On a computer or tablet, the navigation is always shown at the left: what you can browse (Home, then Library,
Authors, Series and Tags as they arrive, marked **Soon** until then) and, at its foot, **Administration**.

On a phone, the **menu** button at the top left opens it as a panel rising from the bottom, with your libraries first
(slide the row sideways when there are many).
The account menu opens the same way. Tap the dimmed page, press Esc, or pick an entry to close it.

## The administration

The administration is a page of its own: the navigation of the library gives way to the administration's sections, and
the arrow at the top left brings you back to where you were in the library.

## Messages and questions

- Before anything is deleted or removed, Librarion asks in a box over the page (on a phone, rising from the bottom).
  **Cancel** is chosen at first, so pressing Enter changes nothing. Deleting a tenant also asks you to type its name.
- Short messages ("Saved.", "Scan started") appear in a corner and go away by themselves. Point at them to keep them,
  or close them with their × button.
