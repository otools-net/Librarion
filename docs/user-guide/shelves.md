# Shelves

Shelves are your own lists of books, in the order you choose: books to read next, favourites, a course's reading
list. Only you see your shelves. A shelf can hold books from any of your libraries. You need the **Own shelves**
right (Users, Curators and Administrators have it).

## Putting books on a shelf

- On a book's page, **Add to shelf** lists your shelves with a box each; a tick means the book is on that shelf. Tick
  a box to put it on, clear it to take it off. **New shelf** makes a shelf and puts the book on it.
- In the library (or on an author's, a tag's, a folder's or a search's books), select books (see
  [Selecting and tagging books](browsing.md#selecting-and-tagging-books)), then **Add to shelf** in the bar at the
  bottom: tick a shelf to put them all on it. Books already on it stay where they are. Up to 1,000 books at a time.
- New books go at the end of the shelf. A shelf holds up to 10,000 books, and you can have up to 100 shelves (an
  administrator can change that number: the setting **Shelves per account**).

## Your shelves

- **Shelves** in the navigation lists your shelves with how many books each holds; **All shelves** opens the
  Shelves page, where **New shelf** makes one.
- A shelf's page shows its books as covers or a list, like the library. Its address reads like it,
  `/shelves/12/To read`; the number finds it, the name is only for reading.
- **Sort** shows the books in the **shelf order** (yours), or by **title**, **author** or **date added** (the last
  put on first).
- A shelf shows only the books you may see now: a book of a library you can no longer open, or hidden from your
  group, is left out (it comes back if you may see it again). Its count may then be higher than the books shown.
- When books are merged as duplicates, a shelf keeps the book they were merged into, in the same place.

## Changing the order

In the shelf order, each book has a grip (six dots) at its top right corner. A selected book moves with the
whole selection.

- **Drag** the grip to where the book should go (with a mouse, a pen or a finger); a line shows where it lands.
- **Click or tap** the grip for **Move**: **To the start**, **Up one**, **Down one**, **To the end**. It stays open
  so you can move a book a few places, saying its place on the shelf.
- With the keyboard, on the grip: the **arrow keys** move it one place, **Home** to the start, **End** to the end.

## Taking books off, renaming, deleting

- Select books on the shelf's page, then **Remove from shelf**: they leave the shelf, not their libraries.
- **Rename** gives the shelf another name (each of your shelves has a name of its own).
- **Delete** removes the shelf and its order, after asking; its books stay in their libraries.
